# Level 3 Support - Linux Engineer

STAND 8 Technology Services

Englewood Cliffs, NJ

## About the job

STAND 8 provides end to end IT solutions to enterprise partners across the United States with offices in Los Angeles, New York, New Jersey, Atlanta and more, including internationally in Mexico, India and Japan.

As a Level 3 Support - Linux Engineer, you will be responsible for providing advanced technical support for complex Linux-based systems. You will handle escalated issues, work on high-level problem resolution, and contribute to the optimization of our infrastructure. This role requires a deep understanding of Linux systems, strong problem-solving skills, and the ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment.

- Develop & maintain engagements with Infrastructure Engineering, MSP resources, facility support, vendors, and Application teams to provide end-to-end delivery and support of appropriate infrastructure solutions.
- Provide level 1/2 operational support for ticket completion, incident management and problem management resolution, including participation within a 24x7 on-call rotation
- Demonstrate a dedication to building and maintaining a stable, high-performance technical environment
- Embrace your role as the ultimate responsible party and senior subject matter expert for technology.

**Client management**

- Strong customer service skills and unfailing professionalism

- Work closely with Business Engagement, operations teams, clients and executive management both formally and informally
- Track timelines, manage priorities and schedules, and set client expectations

- Strong customer service skills and unfailing professionalism
