# Linux System Engineer (Remote)

O’Reilly Auto Parts

Chicago, IL 

**$105K/yr-$130K/yr**

## About the job

A **Linux *System Engineer*** is a highly skilled Linux administrator with knowledge of SOLR, Redis, and/or Apache Kafka. This team member will work collaboratively to develop systems to support the business. Engineers are primarily focused on the design or integration of various systems, application servers, databases and third-party packages.

**The range for this role is $104,000 to 130,000. Most offers for this will be at midpoint around 115 to 120K. This role has an 8% bonus as well.**

**Essential Job Functions**

Take a leadership role in guiding projects through the complete implementation life cycle.

Apply advanced knowledge of Linux administration and platform automation including the underlying theories.

Diagnose issues, build infrastructure, and work with related software.

Apply technical expertise across various client environments and solve any obstacles that arise.

Interact with both internal and external customers during all project phases, from planning to maintenance.

Be mindful of customer needs and clearly communicate complex information.

Demonstrate a high attention to detail, examining every aspect of the system to guarantee security and spot bugs or anomalous activity while they're minor.

Exercise strong time management skills by taking on multiple projects simultaneously while debugging issues reported by customers.

**Skills and Qualifications**

**Required:**

Advanced understanding of Linux.

**Experience with SOLR, Redis, and/or Apache Kafka**

Experience working with **automation tools such as Ansible or Puppet**.

Experience using version control tools such as Git.

Experience **implementing and managing middleware (Tomcat, JBoss, etc.)**

Ability to articulate advanced technical concepts and teach others.

Expert software debugging and testing knowledge.

Advanced knowledge of system services, database systems, and systems analysis and design.
